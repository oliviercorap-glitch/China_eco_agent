Executive summary
China's macro environment for TLD's manufacturing and GSE operations is mixed but net favorable on input costs, with steel prices under sustained downward pressure and lithium collapsed below RMB 100,000/tonne dramatically improving electric GSE economics. However, the demand side remains cautious with manufacturing PMI hovering near 50, air traffic recovery strong domestically but lagging internationally at 80-90% of pre-pandemic levels, and airport infrastructure investment providing a robust multi-year pipeline. The PBOC is managing liquidity smoothly with no near-term tightening, and CNY weakness versus EUR enhances TLD's export competitiveness. The principal risks are escalating US-China trade and tech tensions, property sector weakness dragging on regional government fiscal capacity, and deflationary PPI pressure compressing industrial margins. Priority actions are to lock in favorable steel and lithium input costs, accelerate electric GSE commercial positioning, and stress-test the order book against airport capex deferral scenarios.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Net manufacturing margin outlook for the next 30-90 days is favorable, with steel and aluminium prices stable to declining and lithium costs at multi-year lows reducing electric GSE bill of materials by an estimated 15-25% versus 2022. Deflationary PPI pressure and potential trade-related supply chain disruptions are the primary offsetting risks, but current input cost tailwinds should support gross margin expansion of 1-3% on steel-intensive product lines.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is moderately positive, supported by domestic air traffic exceeding pre-2019 levels, a robust airport construction pipeline including Chengdu Tianfu and Guangzhou Baiyun expansions, and improved electric GSE economics from cheap lithium. International traffic recovery at 80-90% of pre-pandemic levels represents deferred demand that could unlock wide-body GSE orders over the next 12-18 months, though trade tensions and fiscal constraints pose downside risks.

Watch 📊 PMI

China manufacturing PMI hovers near 50, weak but not collapsing

Reading
The official NBS manufacturing PMI has oscillated between roughly 49.0 and 50.8 through 2024, hovering near the boom-bust line of 50, reflecting weak but not collapsing factory activity. The Caixin PMI has been slightly stronger, indicating export-oriented and smaller manufacturers are outperforming the broader index.
Manufacturing impact
A PMI near 50 means TLD's China factories face neither strong tailwinds nor severe headwinds in input availability and pricing, with steel and component supply chains functioning normally. However, sub-50 readings signal demand weakness that could translate into pricing pressure on TLD's GSE products from domestic competitors seeking to fill capacity.
GSE demand impact
PMI below 52 means customer capex unlock is not yet triggered, and GSE order flow from Chinese airport operators and handlers remains cautious. A sustained move above 52 would signal expansion and likely accelerate GSE procurement; a drop below 48 would signal contraction and deferrals.
Recommended action
Monitor the next NBS and Caixin PMI releases for a break above 52 or below 48; if either threshold is breached for the first time in 3+ months, escalate to CRITICAL and convene a demand review within 48 hours.

Important ✈️ AIR_TRAFFIC

China domestic air traffic exceeds pre-2019 levels, international recovery lags at 80-90%

Reading
China's domestic passenger traffic has fully recovered to pre-2019 levels, with 2024 volumes exceeding 2019 by a modest margin, while international traffic recovery has lagged at roughly 80-90% of pre-pandemic levels due to slower visa normalization and geopolitical factors. Air cargo has been a bright spot, outperforming passenger segments.
Manufacturing impact
Strong domestic traffic supports steady demand for domestic-focused GSE such as baggage handling, passenger stairs, and tugs, while lagging international traffic may delay wide-body GSE demand tied to international terminal operations. Air cargo strength supports demand for cargo-specific GSE like container loaders and pallet transporters.
GSE demand impact
Domestic traffic growth above 5% YoY signals handlers need fleet refresh, creating near-term GSE order opportunities. International traffic at 80-90% of pre-pandemic levels represents a deferred demand pool that could unlock significant wide-body GSE orders as recovery completes over the next 12-18 months.
Recommended action
Sales team should prioritize domestic-focused GSE opportunities in the next 90 days while preparing a pipeline strategy for international terminal GSE as visa normalization and route recovery accelerate into 2027.
