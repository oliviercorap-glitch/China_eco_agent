China manufacturing PMI rebounds to 49.8 in August, still below expansion

Reading

Manufacturing impact

The improving trend from July's 49.2 low suggests the manufacturing downturn is bottoming out, supporting stable production planning at Shanghai and Wuxi facilities.
GSE demand impact
PMI below 50 for the third consecutive month indicates customers in the aviation ground handling sector remain cautious about capex commitments. However, the upward trajectory from 49.2 to 49.8 suggests the worst may be passing, and a move above 50 in coming months would unlock deferred GSE procurement.
