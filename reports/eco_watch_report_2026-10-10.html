Executive summary
China's economic picture entering Q4 2026 shows moderate growth with a 4.5-5% GDP target, continued monetary easing expected (20bp rate cut, 50bp RRR cut), and RMB 4.4 trillion in special bonds driving infrastructure investment. Air passenger throughput grew 4.8% in 2025, just below the 5% strong-demand threshold, while US tariffs at 145% remain a significant headwind for China-origin exports to the US market. The EU trade de-escalation on hybrid vehicles reduces broader tariff risk for TLD's European GSE exports, a meaningful positive. CNY has strengthened 2.5% against USD but remains below the 3% EUR threshold that would trigger a critical alert. Net impact on TLD China is moderately positive for domestic and European demand, offset by US market access challenges and compliance cost increases.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins should remain stable over the next 30-90 days with steel prices showing no clear threshold breach, energy costs contained, and PBOC easing reducing financing costs. The primary margin risk comes from tariff-driven component cost inflation (3-7% on electronics) and potential CNY appreciation eroding export competitiveness. Electric GSE margins could improve if battery costs decline from policy-supported electrification investment.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is moderately positive, supported by RMB 4.4 trillion special bond infrastructure spending, 4.8% air traffic growth, and EU trade de-escalation. Domestic airport expansion projects funded by accelerated Q3 special bond issuance should generate 200-400 units of GSE demand in late 2026 and 2027. US market demand from China production faces significant tariff headwinds with potential 15-25% volume loss.
