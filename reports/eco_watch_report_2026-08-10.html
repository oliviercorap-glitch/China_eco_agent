Executive summary
China's economy is growing at 4.7% in H1 2026, within the official 4.5-5% target but at the slowest quarterly pace in three years, with Q2 at 4.3%. Manufacturing PMI at 50.3 signals a third month of expansion, driven by high-tech exports, while equipment manufacturing grows 9.3% and high-tech manufacturing 13.3%. Critical cost pressure comes from lithium prices surging 70% YTD to 140,950 RMB/ton, adding 3-5% to electric GSE manufacturing costs, partially offset by steel prices near multi-year lows around 3,000 RMB/ton and CNY depreciation to 7.9-8.0 per EUR boosting export competitiveness. Policy tailwinds include the NEV heavy-duty truck electrification mandate targeting 40% penetration by 2030, extended US tariff suspension through November 2026, and sustained airport infrastructure investment. Key risks include potential GDP growth overstatement, consumer spending weakness, and new supply chain compliance regulations. Priority actions include locking in steel procurement at low prices, renegotiating battery costs, and positioning TLD for airport electrification programs.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Net manufacturing margin outlook for the next 30-90 days is moderately negative due to lithium-driven battery cost increases of 10-15%, adding 3-5% to electric GSE COGS. This is partially offset by low steel prices near 3,000 RMB/ton and CNY depreciation of 3-4% against EUR, which improves export margins. Overall, expect 1-2% margin compression on electric products unless pricing adjustments are implemented.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is positive, supported by PMI expansion above 50, record air passenger traffic, sustained airport infrastructure investment, and the heavy-duty truck electrification mandate. The 30.8bn EUR airport infrastructure investment pipeline and policy support for electrification should drive GSE procurement, particularly for electric equipment. However, slower GDP growth and consumer spending weakness may temper the pace of demand acceleration.

Critical 🔋 LITHIUM

ALERT: Lithium surged 70% YTD, now at 140,950 RMB/ton

Reading
Battery-grade lithium carbonate is trading at 140,950 RMB/ton as of August 7, 2026, up from approximately 120,000 RMB/ton at the start of the year. The price peaked at 209,800 RMB/ton in May before pulling back, driven by supply disruptions from Jiangxi mine closures, inventory drawdown, and strong lithium iron phosphate production demand.
Manufacturing impact
Lithium price surge of 70% YTD directly impacts TLD's electric GSE battery costs. Batteries represent 20-30% of electric GSE unit cost. A 70% lithium price increase translates to roughly 10-15% higher battery costs, adding 3-5% to total electric GSE manufacturing costs. This is a significant margin pressure point.
GSE demand impact
Higher lithium prices increase the cost of electric GSE, potentially slowing the transition from diesel to electric equipment. However, diesel ban policies and carbon reduction mandates continue to drive electrification demand, creating a cost-push versus policy-pull tension.
Recommended action
Immediately review electric GSE pricing strategy to reflect battery cost increases. Negotiate with battery suppliers for fixed-price contracts or volume discounts. Consider passing through 3-5% price increases on electric models effective Q4 2026.
