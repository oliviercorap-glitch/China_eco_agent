Reading
Lithium carbonate prices have not yet moved meaningfully despite the latest hold-up at CATL's mine, but analysts warn of potential volatility ahead. The situation creates an uncertain supply picture for battery-grade lithium in the near term.
Manufacturing impact
TLD's electric GSE product line depends on lithium battery inputs; a supply disruption or price spike would raise COGS on electric units manufactured in Shanghai and Wuxi. Current price stability offers no immediate margin pressure, but the risk premium on battery sourcing should be reviewed.
