Executive summary
China's economy in late 2026 presents a mixed picture for TLD Group, with strong air traffic growth in early 2026 decelerating to 3.9% by July, steel prices falling on weak demand, and the PBOC maintaining a moderately loose but cautious monetary stance with no rate cuts for 15 consecutive months. The 15th Five-Year Plan's airport cluster upgrade programme across four regions represents a multi-year GSE demand pipeline potentially worth several thousand units, while the US-China tariff deal waiving duties on USD 30 billion of goods improves TLD's competitiveness in the US market. However, the sharp deceleration in air traffic growth from 12.1% in March to 3.9% in July, combined with analyst warnings that 2026 could be China's weakest growth year since 1991, signals caution for H2 2026 order intake. The RMB's appreciation to near 52-week highs against the USD creates FX headwinds for US-denominated revenue but reduces imported input costs. Priority actions include securing GSE framework agreements for the Yangtze Delta and Greater Bay Area airport clusters, quantifying the tariff deal's landed cost benefit, and stress-testing the China revenue plan against a downside GDP scenario.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins should see modest relief over the next 30-90 days from falling steel and iron ore prices, with rebar at multi-month lows potentially reducing steel-heavy product COGS by 3-5%. However, RMB appreciation against the USD may offset some of this benefit on export contracts, and stable interest rates mean no reduction in financing costs. Net margin outlook is cautiously positive but dependent on whether steel price declines are sustained and whether CNY strength moderates.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand for the next 90-180 days is supported by the 15th Five-Year Plan airport cluster programme and the March 2026 air traffic surge, but the July deceleration to 3.9% growth introduces downside risk. The US-China tariff deal could unlock deferred US handler orders in the near term, while domestic demand depends on whether airport investment accelerates in H2 2026. Overall demand outlook is stable to modestly positive, with the Yangtze Delta cluster representing the most immediate opportunity given TLD's manufacturing proximity.
