Executive summary
China's manufacturing sector contracted in July with PMI at 49.2, the first sub-50 reading in six months, while air passenger traffic fell 6.3% year-on-year in June, breaching the critical 5% threshold that triggers GSE demand deferrals. H1 GDP growth of 4.7% missed the 5% target, and household and corporate deleveraging continues with the debt-to-GDP ratio falling to 308.2%. However, structural supports remain: airport infrastructure investment stays elevated at RMB 120 billion annually, aerospace manufacturing grew 18.2%, and lithium prices have corrected 18% from April peaks, improving electric GSE economics. The US-China trade truce holds through November 2026, providing export stability, while the Politburo's restrained stimulus stance limits near-term demand acceleration. Net impact on TLD China is negative for the next 2-3 quarters on the demand side, but input costs remain manageable with stable steel and aluminium prices.

Critical 📊 PMI

ALERT: China manufacturing PMI falls to 49.2 in July, first contraction since January

Reading
The official manufacturing PMI dropped to 49.2 in July 2026, down from expansion territory, marking the first sub-50 reading in six months. This confirms cooling growth momentum and renewed pressure on Beijing to ramp up stimulus support. The decline follows a pattern of weak demand signals seen in January (49.3) and February (49.0) before a brief March recovery.
Manufacturing impact
A PMI below 50 signals contracting factory activity, which typically softens input cost pressures including steel and aluminium. For TLD's Shanghai and Wuxi plants, this may ease near-term raw material cost inflation but also signals weaker industrial demand across the supply chain.
GSE demand impact
PMI at 49.2 is below the 50 threshold but above the 48 critical level, indicating moderate demand weakness rather than severe contraction. Airport and airline customers will likely defer non-essential GSE capex, though the impact is manageable at this level.
Recommended action
CFO should model a 5-10% downside scenario for China GSE orders over next 2 quarters and review inventory buffers for finished goods. Monitor next month's PMI for further deterioration toward 48.

Critical ✈️ AIR_TRAFFIC

ALERT: China June passenger throughput drops 6.3% YoY, breaching 5% critical threshold

Reading
China's civil aviation passenger throughput reached 114 million in June 2026, down 6.3% year-on-year. Passenger volume fell 6.5% to 57.27 million, while total transport turnover grew just 0.2%. Cargo throughput grew 1.1%, providing partial offset. This is the first major traffic decline in the current cycle.
Manufacturing impact
No direct manufacturing cost impact, but the traffic decline signals weaker airport activity and reduced GSE utilization, which will pressure handlers' capex budgets.
GSE demand impact
CRITICAL for GSE demand — a 6.3% traffic drop exceeds the 5% threshold that triggers customer purchase deferrals. Ground handlers will likely postpone fleet refreshes and new equipment orders. TLD should expect order delays of 3-6 months from Chinese airport customers and handlers.
Recommended action
Sales team should proactively engage existing customers to understand capex deferral plans, and CFO should stress-test Q3-Q4 2026 China order book with a 15-20% downside scenario.
