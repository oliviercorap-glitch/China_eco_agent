Executive summary
China's economy is slowing with Q2 2026 GDP at 4.3% and fixed-asset investment contracting 6.7%, but export strength and a 5.5% rise in civil aviation passenger volume to 770 million provide a partial offset for TLD's GSE demand outlook. The most urgent margin threat is aluminium, where LME prices have hit multi-year highs on a 591,000-tonne global deficit and sub-1Mt inventories, with steel also firming regionally. The US-China trade truce extension to January 2027 provides near-term tariff stability, but the November 2026 activation of the affiliate penetration rule creates a hard compliance deadline for TLD's China entities. CNY appreciation toward 6.7 per USD adds export margin headwind, while SOE reform and data security regulations increase operational complexity. Priority actions are immediate aluminium hedging, FX hedge review, and affiliate mapping for US export controls.

Net margin outlook for the next 30-90 days is negative 1-3% unless hedging and price escalation clauses are executed immediately.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)

ALERT — LME aluminium hits multi-year high as China smelter capacity caps out

Reading
Chinese electrolytic aluminium capacity has effectively reached its government-mandated ceiling while overseas capacity expansion remains difficult, pushing LME aluminium to multi-year highs. Global primary aluminium deficit for 2025 is estimated at 591,000 tonnes, with China consumption growth of 2.6% and global demand growth of 2.5%. LME inventories fell below 1 million tonnes for the first time since May 2008.
Manufacturing impact

Every 10% aluminium increase translates to approximately 2-4% COGS impact on aluminium-intensive GSE models, directly compressing gross margin unless passed through.
GSE demand impact
Higher aluminium costs raise GSE unit prices industry-wide, which may slow handler fleet refresh decisions in price-sensitive APAC markets, but also strengthens the case for TLD to lock in long-term supply agreements ahead of competitors.
Recommended action
CFO to authorize immediate hedging or forward purchase of aluminium requirements for Q4 2026 and Q1 2027 within 48 hours, and instruct commercial team to prepare a 3-5% price escalation clause for new GSE quotations.
