Executive summary
China's economy is navigating a challenging period with H1 2026 GDP growth of 4.7% falling below the 5% target midpoint, while the July manufacturing PMI dropped to 49.2, signalling contraction for the first time in months. The property sector crisis continues to weigh on local government finances, though central government special bond issuance at 76% of the annual quota is supporting infrastructure projects including airport expansion. The most significant development for TLD is the 8.94% year-on-year CNY depreciation against the EUR, which dramatically improves the cost competitiveness of China-manufactured GSE exports and provides a substantial margin buffer. However, the PMI contraction and weak domestic demand signal potential headwinds for domestic GSE orders, while US tariffs at 22.8% continue to limit US market access. Priority actions include capturing the FX tailwind in export pricing, monitoring Q4 special bond issuance for airport project acceleration, and ensuring compliance with China's new data security regulations.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins are expected to improve in the next 30-90 days, primarily driven by the 8.94% CNY depreciation against the EUR which reduces effective production costs for export-bound GSE by nearly 9% in EUR terms. Declining lithium prices are reducing electric GSE battery costs by 3-5%, further supporting margins on electrified products. However, the PMI contraction at 49.2 signals potential pricing pressure from competitors and customers, which may partially offset these favourable cost dynamics.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is mixed, with domestic demand at risk from PMI contraction and weak economic confidence, but export demand significantly boosted by CNY depreciation. The CAAC's 10 international aviation hub programme and accelerated special bond issuance provide a strong pipeline of airport infrastructure projects that will drive GSE procurement. Cargo handling equipment demand is likely to outperform passenger GSE given the "stable passenger, fast cargo" aviation sector pattern.

Critical 📊 PMI

ALERT: China manufacturing PMI falls to 49.2 in July, first sub-50 breach in 3+ months

Reading
China's official manufacturing PMI dropped to 49.2 in July 2026 from 50.3 in June, signalling a contraction in both supply and demand. Extreme weather events and weak domestic demand were cited as primary drivers, with production and new orders sub-indices both deteriorating.
Manufacturing impact
A sub-50 PMI signals contraction in TLD's Chinese manufacturing supply chain, potentially easing input cost pressures but also indicating weaker order books for industrial suppliers. Factory utilisation rates across the Yangtze Delta may decline, reducing competition for skilled labour and slightly easing wage inflation.
GSE demand impact
GSE demand is at risk as airport operators and ground handlers facing weaker economic confidence may defer fleet renewal and expansion capex. A PMI below 48 sustained for 2-3 months would typically precede a 10-15% decline in GSE order intake from Chinese customers.
Recommended action
CFO should stress-test Q4 revenue forecasts against a scenario of 10-15% lower domestic GSE orders and review inventory levels to avoid overstock. Operations team should assess whether reduced factory utilisation creates capacity for accelerated export orders.

Critical 💱 FX

ALERT: CNY depreciates 8.94% vs EUR year-on-year, 5.86% vs USD

Reading
EUR/CNY stands at 7.6106 as of 01 October 2026, with the yuan having depreciated 8.94% against the euro over the past year and 5.86% against the dollar. This significant depreciation reflects divergent monetary policies between the PBOC and ECB, as well as capital flow dynamics.
Manufacturing impact
The 8.94% CNY depreciation vs EUR dramatically improves TLD China's cost competitiveness for euro-denominated exports, effectively reducing manufacturing costs by nearly 9% in EUR terms. This provides a substantial margin buffer against input cost inflation and creates opportunities to gain market share from European-based competitors.
GSE demand impact
TLD's China-manufactured GSE becomes significantly more price-competitive in European and other export markets, potentially unlocking orders from price-sensitive handlers in APAC and Europe. However, the depreciation also increases the CNY cost of any EUR-denominated components or licensing fees TLD China pays to its European parent.
Recommended action
CFO should immediately review export pricing strategy to capture the FX tailwind, potentially offering competitive pricing to win market share while maintaining EUR margins. Treasury team should evaluate hedging strategy for EUR-denominated receivables given the 3%+ move threshold breach.
