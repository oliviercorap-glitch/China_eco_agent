PBOC rules out competitive devaluation as EU trade talks open

Reading
The People's Bank of China issued a formal policy statement on 8 October 2026 affirming that market forces will play a decisive role in CNY exchange rate formation, explicitly stating China has no need or intention to devalue the yuan for trade advantage and never engages in competitive devaluation. Manufacturing impact

Procurement of imported components (bearings, controllers, specialty steel) remains cost-neutral absent a sharp move.
GSE demand impact
Currency stability supports predictable pricing for TLD's export order book into Europe and APAC, reducing FX hedging costs for customers structuring multi-year leasing contracts. No immediate demand shift, but reduced FX uncertainty marginally supports cross-border GSE leasing deal flow.
