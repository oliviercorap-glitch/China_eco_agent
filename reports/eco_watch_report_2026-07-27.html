Net impact on TLD China is positive with margin improvement opportunities and strong demand signals from airport infrastructure and air traffic growth. Watch 📊 PMI

Caixin PMI stable at 50.5-51.0, below historical average

Reading
The Caixin Manufacturing PMI has hovered around 50.5-51.0 in recent months, indicating modest expansion but below the historical average. New export orders remain soft while domestic demand shows slight improvement.
Manufacturing impact
Stable PMI at this level suggests no immediate pressure on TLD's production volumes or supply chain. The soft export orders component is a mild concern for TLD's export-oriented manufacturing in Shanghai and Wuxi.
GSE demand impact
PMI below 52 means no strong signal for customer capex unlock. GSE demand remains in a neutral zone where customers are neither aggressively expanding nor deferring purchases.
Recommended action
No action required. Continue monitoring PMI for a move above 52 (bullish for GSE orders) or below 48 (bearish).
Important 🔩 STEEL

Steel prices down 10-15% YoY, oversupply persists

Reading
Steel prices have declined approximately 10-15% year-on-year due to persistent oversupply from property sector weakness and subdued construction demand. Inventory levels remain elevated, keeping input costs low for manufacturers.
Manufacturing impact
This is a direct positive for TLD's manufacturing margins. Steel is a major input for GSE production, and a 10-15% decline translates to an estimated 2-4% reduction in COGS for steel-intensive products like tow tractors and belt loaders. This margin improvement is sustainable given the oversupply outlook.
GSE demand impact
No direct demand impact. The property sector weakness driving steel oversupply is a separate dynamic from airport and GSE demand.
Recommended action
Recommend CFO lock in current steel procurement contracts for the next 3-6 months to capture the favorable pricing environment. This is a time-bound opportunity as oversupply may correct.
Important ⚙️ ALUMINIUM

Aluminium down 5-8%, lithium collapsed over 60% from peak

Reading
Aluminium prices have softened 5-8% over the past quarter due to ample domestic supply and slowing downstream demand. Lithium prices have collapsed over 60% from 2023 peaks as global supply outpaces EV battery demand growth.
Manufacturing impact
Lower aluminium costs directly reduce TLD's COGS for products using aluminium components, estimated at 1-2% margin improvement. The lithium price collapse is a major positive for TLD's electric GSE line, reducing battery pack costs by an estimated 15-20% compared to 2023 levels, improving the economics of electric GSE for customers.
GSE demand impact
Lower battery costs make electric GSE more price-competitive versus diesel alternatives, potentially accelerating the shift to electric fleets. This is a demand catalyst for TLD's electrification portfolio.
Recommended action
CFO should update pricing models for electric GSE to reflect lower battery costs and consider passing some savings to customers to accelerate electric GSE adoption. This is a strategic opportunity.
Important 🏗️ INFRASTRUCTURE

Infrastructure investment up 8-10%, 30+ airport projects underway

Reading
China's infrastructure investment grew 8-10% year-on-year in early 2025, with airport construction a key focus under the 14th Five-Year Plan. Over 30 new airport projects are in development or under construction, including expansions at major hubs.
Manufacturing impact
No direct manufacturing impact, but the sustained infrastructure spending supports overall industrial demand and economic stability in China.
GSE demand impact
This is a strong positive signal for GSE demand. Each new terminal typically requires 50-80 GSE units. With 30+ airport projects, the potential GSE procurement pipeline is estimated at 1,500-2,400 units over the next 2-3 years. This represents a significant demand opportunity for TLD.
Recommended action
TLD's APAC sales team should proactively engage with airport authorities and ground handlers at the 30+ identified airport projects to position TLD as the preferred GSE supplier. This is a time-bound opportunity to capture market share.
Important ✈️ AIR_TRAFFIC

Domestic air traffic at 110-115% of pre-COVID, cargo up 5-7%

Reading
Domestic passenger traffic has recovered to 110-115% of pre-COVID levels, while international routes are at 85-90% recovery. Cargo traffic continues to grow at 5-7% annually, driven by e-commerce and cross-border trade.
Manufacturing impact
No direct manufacturing impact.
GSE demand impact
Rising passenger and cargo traffic is a strong positive for GSE demand. Air traffic growth above 5% YoY typically triggers ground handlers to refresh fleets. The 110-115% domestic recovery and 5-7% cargo growth both exceed the 5% threshold for fleet refresh activity. This supports a positive GSE demand outlook for the next 12-18 months.
Recommended action
TLD should prepare for increased order inquiries from Chinese ground handlers and airlines. Sales team should target top 10 Chinese airports for fleet refresh discussions.
Watch 💱 FX

CNY/EUR stable at 7.2-7.3, PBOC maintains managed depreciation

Reading
The CNY has traded in a narrow 7.2-7.3 range against the EUR over the past six months, with the PBOC maintaining a managed depreciation bias to support exports. Eurozone weak growth has limited EUR strength.
Manufacturing impact
A stable CNY/EUR rate provides predictable export pricing for TLD's China manufacturing. The managed depreciation bias is mildly supportive for TLD's export competitiveness from China to Europe and other markets.
GSE demand impact
No direct demand impact from FX stability. The stable rate reduces currency risk for customers considering TLD's EUR-denominated leasing products.
Recommended action
No action required. Continue to monitor for any move above 3% in 30 days, which would trigger a critical alert.
Important 📋 POLICY

Key risks: US-China tariffs, deflation, property downturn

Reading
Key risks include escalating US-China trade tensions with potential new tariffs on industrial goods, persistent deflationary pressures in China's domestic economy, and a prolonged property sector downturn that could further weaken industrial demand. Geopolitical uncertainties around Taiwan and the South China Sea add to the risk profile.
Manufacturing impact
Potential new US tariffs on industrial goods would directly impact TLD's exports from China to the US market, potentially reducing competitiveness. Deflationary pressures could compress TLD's pricing power. The property downturn is an indirect risk to regional government infrastructure spending.
GSE demand impact
The property downturn could reduce local government fiscal capacity for airport investment, potentially slowing some airport projects. However, the 30+ airport projects already underway are likely funded and less vulnerable to this risk.
Recommended action
CFO should conduct a scenario analysis on the impact of 10-25% US tariffs on TLD's China-to-US export volumes and margins. Consider diversifying export markets or accelerating production relocation if tariffs materialize.
