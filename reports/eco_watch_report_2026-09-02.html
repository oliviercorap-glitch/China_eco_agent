Executive summary
China's economy is decelerating with H1 GDP at 4.7% and Q2 slowing to 4.3%, while the official manufacturing PMI has fallen below 50 to 49.2, signaling contraction in factory activity. Air passenger traffic growth of only 1% in H1 2026 remains well below the 5% threshold needed to drive GSE fleet expansion, though July's 3.9% recovery and the 15th Five-Year Plan's massive airport construction mandate provide counterbalancing demand catalysts. The RMB 400 billion plus airport infrastructure pipeline represents a potential 550-1,000 GSE unit opportunity for TLD over the plan period. Manufacturing input costs remain manageable with stable steel prices and moderate aluminium increases, while PBOC's expected RRR cut will improve financing conditions for both TLD and its customers. The CNY has appreciated 2.7% against the EUR since February, pressuring export competitiveness, and US tariff uncertainty persists with the November 2026 deadline approaching. Net assessment: near-term demand softness balanced by strong medium-term infrastructure-driven GSE pipeline, with margin outlook stable but requiring active cost management.

GSE demand impact

Critical 🏗️ INFRASTRUCTURE

Reading

GSE demand impact
Each new terminal represents 50-80 GSE units; with 3 new airports and 8 major expansions planned, this translates to a potential pipeline of 550-1,000+ GSE units over the plan period. This is a multi-year demand catalyst for TLD's electric and conventional GSE portfolio.
