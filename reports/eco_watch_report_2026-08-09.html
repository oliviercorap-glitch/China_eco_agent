Executive summary
China's macro environment in August 2026 presents a mixed but manageable picture for TLD's manufacturing operations. Steel prices have declined approximately 10% from May highs, providing meaningful COGS relief for GSE production, while PPI growth slowing to 3.5% confirms easing input cost pressures. The PBOC's confirmed moderately loose monetary policy stance with expected rate cuts supports both TLD's financing costs and customer capex, with 10-year government bond yields already declining to 1.71%. Civil aviation continues its strong growth trajectory with 810 million passenger target for 2026, exceeding the 5% growth threshold that signals fleet refresh demand. The US-China trade deal pausing tariffs and export restrictions reduces supply chain uncertainty, while the new battery consumption tax of 2% from September adds modest cost pressure to electric GSE products. The low-altitude economy designation as a pillar industry opens new adjacent market opportunities. Overall, the net impact on TLD China is moderately positive with cost relief from steel offsetting battery tax increases, supported by robust aviation demand fundamentals.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins should improve modestly over the next 30-90 days, driven by approximately 10% lower steel costs offset by the 2% battery consumption tax effective September 1. Energy costs are easing with lower fuel prices, while stable CNY supports predictable export pricing. Net margin impact is estimated at positive 1-2% for steel-intensive products and neutral to slightly negative for electric GSE products.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is positive, supported by record 2025 air traffic levels, the 810 million passenger target for 2026 representing 5-6% growth, and expected PBOC rate cuts that will reduce customer financing costs. The US-China trade deal improves business confidence, while low-altitude economy development creates new market segments. Airport construction continues, though real estate distress may constrain some regional infrastructure spending.

Reading

GSE demand impact
Higher battery costs may slightly increase electric GSE prices, potentially dampening demand for electric conversions. However, the impact is modest relative to total equipment cost.
Recommended action
