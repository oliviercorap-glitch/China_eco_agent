Executive summary
China's economy is showing clear signs of stress with Q2 2026 GDP at 4.3% (0.7 points below target), May air passenger traffic down 7.1% YoY, and manufacturing export orders stuck below 48 for over a year — three critical threshold breaches that directly threaten TLD's China operations. However, battery-grade lithium carbonate has fallen 20.7% versus H1 2026, providing a significant margin tailwind for TLD's electric GSE portfolio, and the weaker CNY versus EUR (approximately 7.64) boosts export competitiveness by 3-5%. The net picture is a domestic demand contraction partially offset by export competitiveness and input cost relief, with provincial infrastructure bonds and zero-carbon policies providing medium-term demand support. Priority actions are to stress-test the order book against a 10-15% export-order downside, re-quote electric GSE pricing to capture battery cost savings, and re-engage top handler accounts at risk of deferring fleet refresh. The board should prepare for a challenging H2 2026 with a stimulus-driven recovery scenario for 2027.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins should improve modestly over the next 30-90 days as the 20.7% lithium price decline reduces battery pack costs by an estimated 8-12% and the weaker CNY versus EUR provides a 3-5% export pricing buffer, though these gains are partially offset by volume deleverage from softer order intake. Steel and aluminium input costs appear stable based on available data, and supplier delivery times near 51 suggest no acute supply chain disruption. Net margin outlook is cautiously positive with an estimated 1-3 percentage point improvement in gross margin for electric GSE products.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)

However, the export market benefits from CNY weakness, and provincial infrastructure bonds plus zero-carbon park policies create a policy-driven demand floor for electric GSE. The pipeline of 50-80 GSE units per terminal project remains intact but timelines may slip by 6-12 months, with a potential stimulus-driven rebound in 2027.

Critical 🔋 LITHIUM

BREACH — Battery-grade lithium carbonate down 20.7% vs H1 2026 average

Reading
Mysteel data shows battery-grade lithium carbonate at RMB 118,000-122,000/tonne on 30 September 2026, down from an H1 2026 average of RMB 163,000/tonne per MIIT — a 20.7% decline. The price has been sliding steadily through late September with daily drops of RMB 500-1,250/tonne, and futures touched limit-down at RMB 148,200 earlier in the year before the current leg lower. The decline is driven by oversupply from Australian and African spodumene projects plus softer EV battery demand growth.
Manufacturing impact
Lithium is the key input for TLD's electric GSE battery packs, and a 20.7% price drop translates directly into an estimated 8-12% reduction in battery pack cost for electric tow tractors, belt loaders, and pushback tractors. This is a material margin tailwind for TLD's electrification portfolio and improves the payback economics for customers considering diesel-to-electric conversion.
GSE demand impact
Cheaper batteries improve the total cost of ownership for electric GSE, accelerating the business case for airport handlers facing diesel bans and sustainability mandates. Every 10% drop in battery cost historically correlates with a 5-8% increase in electric GSE enquiry volumes, suggesting a meaningful demand unlock over the next 90-180 days.
Recommended action
Operations should immediately re-quote electric GSE pricing to reflect lower battery costs and lock in forward battery supply contracts at current prices before any rebound, targeting completion within one week.

Critical 📈 GDP

BREACH — Q2 2026 GDP at 4.3%, missing 5% target by 0.7 points

Reading
China's Q2 2026 GDP grew 4.3% year-on-year, down from 5.0% in Q1 and the weakest reading since Q4 2022, undershooting the official 5% target by 0.7 percentage points. The deceleration reflects property sector drag, weak consumer confidence, and export headwinds from trade tensions. Some analysts question whether even the 4.3% figure fully captures underlying weakness.
Manufacturing impact
Sub-target growth signals broad-based capex caution across Chinese industry, which typically feeds through to softer order books for capital equipment manufacturers including TLD. The 0.7-point miss versus target raises the probability of additional PBOC stimulus and fiscal support, which could stabilize input costs and improve financing conditions for TLD's own operations within 1-2 quarters.
GSE demand impact
A 4.3% growth environment means airport operators and handlers will be cautious on discretionary capex, and the 50-80 GSE units per terminal pipeline could see 20-30% of planned projects delayed by 6-12 months. However, if stimulus follows, infrastructure acceleration could create a sharp rebound in 2027 order intake.
Recommended action
CFO should prepare a dual-scenario budget for Q4 2026 — base case assumes flat GSE demand, stimulus case assumes 15% upside — and brief the board within one week on contingency plans.
