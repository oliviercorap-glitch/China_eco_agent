However, the sharp deceleration in air traffic growth from 12.1% in March to 3.9% in July, combined with analyst warnings that 2026 could be China's weakest growth year since 1991, signals caution for H2 2026 order intake. MARGIN_OUTLOOK: Manufacturing margins should see modest relief over the next 30-90 days from falling steel and iron ore prices, with rebar at multi-month lows potentially reducing steel-heavy product COGS by 3-5%. However, RMB appreciation against the USD may offset some of this benefit on export contracts, and stable interest rates mean no reduction in financing costs. Net margin outlook is cautiously positive but dependent on whether steel price declines are sustained and whether CNY strength moderates.
DEMAND_OUTLOOK: GSE demand for the next 90-180 days is supported by the 15th Five-Year Plan airport cluster programme and the March 2026 air traffic surge, but the July deceleration to 3.9% growth introduces downside risk. The US-China tariff deal could unlock deferred US handler orders in the near term, while domestic demand depends on whether airport investment accelerates in H2 2026. Overall demand outlook is stable to modestly positive, with the Yangtze Delta cluster representing the most immediate opportunity given TLD's manufacturing proximity.
