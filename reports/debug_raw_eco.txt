Net impact on TLD China is positive on cost and export competitiveness, neutral on volume until airport projects convert to orders, and exposed on trade policy. Priority actions are locking forward steel and rare-earth contracts, refreshing the electric GSE TCO model, and running a tariff exposure scenario.
