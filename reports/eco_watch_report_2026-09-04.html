Important 📊 PMI

PMI rises to 49.8 but stays below 50 for second month

Reading
China's official NBS Manufacturing PMI improved to 49.8 in August 2026 from 49.2 in July, slightly beating market expectations. The index remains below the 50 threshold for a second consecutive month, signaling continued contraction in manufacturing activity. New orders reportedly hit multi-year lows, keeping commodity markets on edge.
Manufacturing impact
The sub-50 PMI reading indicates soft industrial demand across China, which may pressure TLD's supplier base on pricing but also signals weaker overall factory utilization. Input costs for steel and other raw materials may ease as downstream demand remains tepid. TLD's Shanghai and Wuxi plants should see stable input pricing in the near term.
GSE demand impact
PMI below 50 for two consecutive months signals customers in the aviation ground handling sector may defer capital expenditure on GSE fleet renewal. However, the improvement from 49.2 to 49.8 suggests the contraction is moderating, and the PMI did not breach the critical 48 threshold. GSE order flow is at risk but not in crisis territory.
Recommended action
CFO should model a conservative Q4 2026 order book scenario assuming PMI stays below 50, while preparing sales incentives for customers who may accelerate orders before year-end budget cycles.

Reading

GSE demand impact

Recommended action
Management should prepare a downside scenario for 2027 orders assuming GDP growth remains below 4.5%, including contingency plans for production scheduling and inventory management.
