Executive summary
China's economy is cooling sharply in August 2026, with industrial output growth at 4.5%, retail sales collapsing to 1.2% growth, and private credit stress building across the financial system. The combination of weak domestic consumption, cooling industrial activity, and global crisis risk creates a challenging environment for TLD's China manufacturing operations. Manufacturing margins face modest pressure from softer input costs, but demand risk is significant as airlines and ground handlers defer capital expenditure. The GSE order pipeline faces downside risk of 10-20% over the next two quarters, requiring immediate scenario planning and inventory adjustments. Priority actions include stress-testing the order book, tightening credit terms, and re-forecasting domestic China demand downward.
