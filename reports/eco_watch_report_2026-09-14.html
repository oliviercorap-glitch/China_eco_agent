Watch 📊 PMI

China manufacturing PMI oscillates 49.0-50.8, hovering at boom-bust line

Reading
The official NBS manufacturing PMI has ranged between roughly 49.0 and 50.8 through 2024, indicating tepid but non-collapsing factory activity with no decisive expansion or contraction. The Caixin PMI reads slightly stronger, suggesting export-oriented and smaller manufacturers are outperforming the state-heavy NBS sample. No single reading breaches the 48 contraction or 52 expansion thresholds.
Manufacturing impact
A PMI near 50 signals flat order books for TLD's Chinese industrial suppliers, limiting input price inflation but also capping volume discounts on steel, aluminium, and components. Stable supplier lead times reduce production scheduling risk at Shanghai and Wuxi plants, supporting predictable COGS within a narrow band.
GSE demand impact
PMI below 52 means TLD's domestic Chinese manufacturing customers are not in a broad capex unlock cycle, so GSE orders from Chinese industrial and logistics clients remain steady rather than surging. Airport and airline customers, driven more by traffic than PMI, are the stronger demand pillar at present.
Recommended action
Maintain current production run-rates and monitor the next two monthly PMI prints; escalate to IMPORTANT if the reading breaks below 48 or above 52 for the first time in three months.
Watch 🔩 STEEL

Chinese steel oversupply keeps prices favorable and stable for TLD procurement

Reading
Chinese crude steel output continues to exceed domestic demand, with exports surging to absorb the surplus, while property-sector weakness has crushed construction steel demand. Infrastructure and manufacturing demand provide only partial offsets, keeping hot-rolled and related steel prices under pressure. No 10% 30-day price move is indicated, so no threshold breach.
Manufacturing impact
Favorable and stable steel pricing directly benefits TLD's GSE chassis, frames, and structural steel fabrication at Shanghai and Wuxi, supporting gross margin retention or modest COGS reduction. The export surplus also means TLD can negotiate favorable terms with domestic mills facing excess inventory, potentially locking in forward pricing below spot.
GSE demand impact
Weak construction steel demand reflects ongoing property-sector drag, which indirectly pressures local government finances and could slow regional airport capex timing. However, the national airport construction pipeline remains prioritized, so GSE demand from aviation infrastructure is not materially impaired by steel market softness.
Recommended action
Lock in 6-12 month forward steel supply contracts at current favorable prices to hedge against any future supply-side policy tightening or production cuts; review mill terms this week.
Watch ⚙️ ALUMINIUM

Aluminium range-bound with mild upward bias on supply caps and EV demand

Reading
SHFE aluminium prices are range-bound with a mild upward bias, supported by China's capacity ceiling of approximately 45Mt and rising demand from EVs and solar. Lithium carbonate has collapsed from 2022 peaks above RMB 500,000/t to below RMB 100,000/t, stabilizing in 2024 as supply caught up with demand. Neither metal shows a threshold breach — aluminium is well under 10% and lithium is stabilizing rather than moving 20% in 30 days.
Manufacturing impact
Mild aluminium upside bias is a modest COGS headwind for TLD's aluminium-intensive GSE components, but the capacity ceiling limits sharp spikes. The lithium price collapse is highly favorable for TLD's electric GSE battery costs, materially improving the economics of electrified ground support equipment and supporting margin expansion on EV product lines.
GSE demand impact
Cheaper lithium directly lowers the purchase price and total cost of ownership of electric GSE, accelerating handler and airport conversion from diesel to electric fleets. This strengthens TLD's electrification portfolio competitiveness against diesel incumbents and aligns with diesel-ban policies in Chinese and APAC airports.
Recommended action
Accelerate quoting and marketing of electric GSE models leveraging the lithium cost advantage; hedge aluminium exposure with forward contracts given the mild upward bias.

GSE demand impact

This FX tailwind supports export order intake and could accelerate market share gains in price-sensitive emerging markets.
Recommended action
CFO to review EUR/CNY hedging policy and consider increasing hedge ratios on expected EUR-denominated revenue; commercial team to emphasize FX-driven price competitiveness in export quotations this quarter.

Recommended action
