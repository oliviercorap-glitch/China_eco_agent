Executive summary
China's economy is in a phase of tentative stabilisation with H1 2026 GDP growth of 4.3% in Q2 and a full-year forecast upgraded to 4.8% by Goldman Sachs, while the manufacturing PMI at 50.3 in June sits just above the expansion threshold. The most critical signal for TLD is the approximately 3% CNY appreciation year-to-date, with EUR/CNY at 7.7777, which directly compresses export margins on China-manufactured GSE and represents a threshold breach requiring immediate hedging action. Steel prices remain range-bound to weak, providing modest COGS relief, while the new Data Security Law imposes severe penalties of up to RMB 10 million for unauthorised cross-border data transfers, creating an urgent compliance obligation for TLD's China entities. The 15th Five-Year Carbon Peak Plan and NEV rural campaign both reinforce the structural tailwind for electric GSE, with battery cost reductions improving the economics of TLD's electrification portfolio. Priority actions are to hedge EUR/CNY exposure within 48 hours, conduct a data security compliance audit immediately, and prepare an electrification sales pitch leveraging the carbon peak policy for Chinese airport operators.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins face a net negative pressure over the next 30-90 days, primarily from the 3% CNY appreciation which effectively raises export prices in EUR terms and compresses translated margins. This is partially offset by flat-to-declining steel prices and falling battery costs, which provide 1-3% COGS relief on electric GSE models. Net margin impact is estimated at negative 1-2% for export-heavy product lines unless price adjustments or hedging are implemented.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is mildly positive but below the strong expansion threshold, with PMI at 50.3 supporting steady-state replacement demand but not the capex surge that occurs above 52. The carbon peak plan and airport infrastructure coordination under the "Six Networks" framework provide a pipeline of potential new terminal projects, each generating 50-80 GSE units, but timing remains uncertain. Electric GSE demand is structurally supported by policy tailwinds and improving battery economics.

Important ⚖️ COMPLIANCE

China's new Data Security Law imposes strict cross-border transfer penalties

Reading
China's Data Security Law prohibits operators from providing domestically stored data to foreign law enforcement or judicial bodies without prior Chinese government approval. Penalties reach up to RMB 10 million (approximately USD 1.56 million) for serious violations, plus business suspension or licence revocation. This significantly affects cross-border data flows for foreign-invested enterprises.
Manufacturing impact
TLD's China entities must ensure all operational data, including supply chain records, production data, and employee information, comply with the new law, potentially requiring localised IT infrastructure investment. Non-compliance risk includes fines up to RMB 10 million and business licence revocation, which would be catastrophic for TLD's Shanghai and Wuxi operations.
GSE demand impact
Compliance burden may slow TLD's ability to share customer and order data across borders, potentially delaying GSE order processing and after-sales support for international customers. However, this is an operational risk rather than a demand driver.
Recommended action
CFO and legal team must conduct an immediate data flow audit across TLD's China entities to identify any cross-border data transfers that require government approval, with remediation plan within 48 hours given the severity of penalties.
