July 2025 data shows domestic passenger throughput of 142.63 million, up 3.2% year-on-year. International route passenger kilometers grew 14.7% in July and 26.5% for the January-July period. Cargo tonne kilometers grew 16.2% in July, with international cargo up 18.0%.
Manufacturing impact
The strong international traffic growth supports demand for wide-body capable GSE equipment. The cargo growth of 16.2% in July indicates robust air cargo activity, supporting demand for cargo handling equipment.
GSE demand impact
International traffic growth of 14.7% in July suggests hub airports are expanding international operations, requiring additional GSE fleets. The strong cargo growth supports demand for cargo-specific GSE equipment.
Recommended action
TLD should target hub airports with strong international and cargo growth for GSE sales. Emphasize wide-body capable and cargo handling equipment in these markets.

China's 2026 monetary policy maintains moderately loose stance with easing tools ready

Reading
The PBOC has confirmed its 2026 monetary policy will maintain a moderately loose stance, with flexible use of RRR cuts and interest rate reductions. The central bank emphasizes supporting domestic demand, technological innovation, and small and medium enterprises. Market indicators show the 7-day reverse repo rate at 1.40% with ample liquidity.
Manufacturing impact
The accommodative monetary stance supports stable financing conditions for TLD's China operations. The policy focus on equipment renewal and SME support may provide opportunities for manufacturing upgrades at favorable financing terms.
GSE demand impact
The moderately loose policy stance supports customer financing for GSE purchases and leasing. The policy emphasis on domestic demand may support airport infrastructure spending and related GSE procurement.
Recommended action
Finance team should monitor for actual rate cuts or RRR reductions and be prepared to adjust leasing rates and financing offers to customers accordingly.
