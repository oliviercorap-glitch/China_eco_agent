Executive summary
China's economy is showing clear signs of deceleration with Q2 GDP growth of 4.3% falling below the official 4.5-5% target range, the weakest quarterly performance since 2022. The government has responded with a massive RMB 400 billion infrastructure approval package including Guangzhou's new airport, which represents a direct GSE procurement opportunity for TLD. Aluminium prices are rising modestly at RMB 23,970 per tonne, adding slight COGS pressure. Air traffic data shows a concerning divergence with passenger growth of only 0.5% while aircraft movements fell 6.2%, suggesting handlers may defer fleet refreshes. Trade tensions are escalating with US polysilicon tariffs and China's first activation of its blocking statute, creating compliance complexity for TLD's cross-border operations. Property sector stabilisation is progressing but remains fragile with continued defaults. Net impact on TLD China is mixed: infrastructure stimulus supports GSE demand, but weak GDP growth and air traffic stagnation pose risks to customer capex.

📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
