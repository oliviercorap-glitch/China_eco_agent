US-China slash tariffs on US$30bn of basic goods, rare earths excluded

This is a meaningful de-escalation in trade tensions, though the exclusion of rare earths signals strategic materials remain contested. The tariff relief covers basic goods rather than core industrial inputs.

GSE demand impact

Lower tariffs on basic goods may also reduce costs for airport construction materials, indirectly supporting infrastructure timelines. The overall easing of tensions is a modest positive for cross-border GSE order flow.
Recommended action
