Important 📋 POLICY

China expands loan interest subsidy program to boost consumption and real economy

Reading
Six major state-owned banks including ICBC, ABC, BOC, CCB, and BoCom have published detailed implementation rules for the new loan interest subsidy policy, covering small and medium enterprises and service sector operators. Personal consumption loan subsidies have been expanded to include all credit card installment purchases.
Manufacturing impact
Cheaper working capital financing for TLD's China operations and its domestic supplier base in the Yangtze Delta region, potentially reducing financing costs by 1-2 percentage points on eligible loans. This improves working capital efficiency for raw material procurement of steel and aluminium components.
GSE demand impact
Subsidized consumer credit supports domestic air travel demand, which underpins GSE utilization rates at Chinese airports. More importantly, cheaper SME financing may unlock capex for smaller ground handling operators who have deferred fleet renewal.
Recommended action
CFO should instruct China treasury team to assess eligibility of TLD China entities and key suppliers for the loan subsidy program within 2 weeks, quantifying potential interest savings.

Watch 🔋 LITHIUM

CATL lithium mine restart uncertainty could fuel price volatility, analysts warn

Reading
The latest hold-up at CATL's lithium mine has yet to meaningfully roil lithium carbonate prices, but analysts warn the impact may be significant if the restart is further delayed. The situation creates uncertainty in the lithium supply chain for battery production.
Manufacturing impact
Lithium carbonate price volatility directly affects TLD's electric GSE battery input costs. A sustained price spike above current levels would increase COGS for electric tow tractors, belt loaders, and cargo loaders manufactured in Shanghai and Wuxi. Current impact is contained but supply chain risk is rising.
GSE demand impact
Higher lithium prices could slow the price convergence of electric GSE versus diesel equipment, potentially delaying customer conversion decisions in price-sensitive APAC markets. However, regulatory diesel bans continue to support electrification demand regardless of battery costs.
Recommended action
Procurement team should secure lithium battery pricing commitments from suppliers for Q4 2026 deliveries within 30 days, and model COGS impact of a 20% lithium carbonate price increase.
