Executive summary
China's economy is in a phase of moderating growth with H1 2026 GDP at 5.0% but Q2 slowing to 4.3% and full-year forecasts at 4.6%, below the official 5% target, raising expectations for additional stimulus. Manufacturing input costs are stable, with rebar at RMB 3,300/tonne and lithium carbonate at RMB 142,000-145,000/tonne, both well within threshold bands, providing COGS certainty for TLD's China operations. Air traffic grew 3.9% in July, below the 5% acceleration threshold, while airport infrastructure continues to expand with 270 airports and RMB 7,818bn in special bond transport funding supporting the GSE pipeline. The yuan has appreciated about 3% against the dollar, a headwind for export competitiveness that requires CFO attention. Net impact on TLD China is neutral-to-cautiously-positive, with stable input costs and a steady GSE demand pipeline offset by moderating GDP growth and currency headwinds. Priority actions are to hedge EUR/CNY exposure, secure battery supply contracts, and map special bond-funded airport projects for GSE sales.
📉 Manufacturing margin outlook (30-90 days)
Manufacturing margins are stable to slightly positive over the next 30-90 days, with steel and lithium prices range-bound and no threshold breaches. The 2% lithium battery consumption tax adds a minor cost layer to electric GSE, but stable lithium prices and high steel inventories keep input cost pressure contained. PPI at plus 1.5% signals mild industrial inflation that could gradually firm costs but is not yet margin-threatening.
📈 GSE demand outlook (90-180 days)
GSE demand outlook for the next 90-180 days is steady but not surging, with air traffic growth at 3.9% below the 5% acceleration threshold and GDP moderating to 4.6%. The RMB 7,818bn special bond transport allocation and 270-airport network expansion provide a solid pipeline, particularly for cargo-handling GSE where throughput grew 9.0%. Stimulus expectations could unlock accelerated airport capex in H2 2026, but near-term customer capex deferral risk is elevated.
