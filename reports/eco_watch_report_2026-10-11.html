RMB 4.4tn special bond quota, only 47% issued by mid-year

Watch 📋 POLICY

NEV subsidy cuts threaten EV supply chain, battery cost outlook uncertain

Reading
A commentary claims 2026 could be a "year of NEV bankruptcies" in China, citing BYD market share halving, first net profit decline in four years, and reduced government subsidies. It also cites USD 1.04 trillion in capital outflow in 2025 and a gap between 5% GDP growth and a 6.5% decline in central fiscal revenue. These are commentary claims, not verified official data.
Manufacturing impact
If NEV subsidies are cut, battery and electric drivetrain costs for TLD's electric GSE could rise or become less predictable. Conversely, consolidation in the EV sector may free up battery cell capacity and reduce prices for industrial electric vehicles. TLD should monitor lithium and battery pack pricing closely.
GSE demand impact
Weaker EV sector health could slow the broader electrification push at Chinese airports, delaying diesel-to-electric GSE conversion timelines. However, airport electrification mandates are policy-driven and may be insulated from consumer EV subsidy cuts.
Recommended action
Procurement team should lock in battery supply contracts for electric GSE within 30 days to hedge against potential NEV supply chain disruption.

Important 📋 POLICY

NEV dual-credit policy mandates 38% NEV credits, drives electrification

Reading
China's NEV dual-credit policy requires automakers to generate 38% NEV credits by 2025, escalating annually, under a cap-and-trade mechanism isolated to automotive manufacturing. This forces ICE manufacturers to subsidise EV production and accelerates the broader electrification ecosystem. Last verified August 2026.
Manufacturing impact
The policy accelerates domestic battery production scale, which should reduce lithium-ion pack costs for TLD's electric GSE over time. It also signals continued government commitment to electrification, reducing regulatory risk for TLD's electric product investments. Supply chain for electric drivetrains and battery management systems becomes more competitive.
GSE demand impact
Airport operators face increasing policy pressure to electrify ground fleets, directly benefiting TLD's electric GSE portfolio. Chinese airports may mandate electric GSE procurement in new terminal projects, creating a structural demand tailwind. This is a multi-year opportunity worth 20-30% of TLD's China GSE pipeline.
Recommended action
Business development should quantify electric GSE pipeline tied to airport electrification mandates and present a 3-year electrification revenue forecast to the board within 45 days.
