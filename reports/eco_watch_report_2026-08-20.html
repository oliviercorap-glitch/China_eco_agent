C919 localization drive signals aerospace import substitution acceleration

Reading
China is intensifying efforts to replace Western aerospace parts in the C919 program, a long-term strategic push that reflects broader import substitution policy across advanced manufacturing. The localization strategy is acknowledged as a multi-year challenge and is not expected to derail the aircraft's production schedule.
Manufacturing impact
For TLD's China operations, this signals a policy environment increasingly favoring domestic supply chains in aerospace-adjacent sectors. While GSE components differ from aircraft parts, the localization push may tighten the regulatory and procurement environment for foreign-invested manufacturers, potentially affecting component sourcing flexibility and supplier relationships in the Yangtze Delta.
GSE demand impact
The C919 program's expansion supports China's broader aviation ecosystem growth, including airport infrastructure and ground handling capacity. As domestic aircraft production scales, associated airport and airline investments are likely to follow, creating a positive medium-term tailwind for GSE procurement pipelines.
Recommended action
